Course: The academic item unto completing a degree.

Instructor: Any academic actors that might oversee course instruction.

Student: Any academic actors that might enroll in course instruction.

Team: A group of students working on an assignment.

Class: A course is taught in a particular semester at some meeting frequency, with so many instructors, and with so many students enrolled.

Assignment: Those academic actions by which a student must complete to grow in his understanding and is graded for demonstration of that understanding.

Attendance: Generally, a pass/fail assessment for whether a student was present for a class meeting or lecture.

Exam: A course milestone as a semi-cumulative assessment of student understanding thus far.

Quiz: A course checkpoint to help students gauge progress and understanding.

Homework: A smaller assessment of student understanding of a few concepts.

Paper: Display through the written word of student understanding of course concepts, research background information and social impacts, and problem solving skills.

Presentation: Similar to paper but displayed through the oral word.

Project: Generally a semester-long whereby students apply all they have learned from the course.

Question: Items of Assignment for students to demonstrate understanding of various concepts.

Rubric: Those metrics by which displays of understanding are measured against some weighted categories.

Point: A numeric evaluation measuring a student's level of understanding of an assignment or question according to some rubric.

Percent: Similar to Point but normalized as a percentage and can be used to distribute weight of rubric categories.

Mark: The encoded red line marks from a student's submitted work.

Evaluation: Couples a Mark to some academic item–Question or Rubric–with ability to record comments/feedback.

Score: Compilation of evaluations across an assignment unto a scalar value whether represented as raw score, percentage earned against value of assignment, or a letter (A, B, C, D, F).

Submission: Coupling of a compiled Score, any Evaluations from the submitted work, and the datetime stamp of the submitted work.

Grade: Explicit connection of a student and his submitted work for some assignment.

Gradebook: Source of truth to include all academic activities for every student and all assignments.
